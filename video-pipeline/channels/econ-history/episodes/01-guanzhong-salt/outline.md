# Outline — EP01 管仲：不加税，也能让国库暴富？ / The Tax Nobody Noticed

Target: ~10 min (EN) · Chinese cut = 3 shorts split at the act breaks.

## Cold open (part 1, ≤30s)
- Hook: a government that takes more of your money but never raises taxes — 2,600 years ago someone did it; the secret is in your kitchen. Salt: you can skip meat, not salt.

## Act 1 — The man who shot a duke (part 1)
1. **Chapter card.**
2. **Story**: 685 BC succession race; Guan Zhong shoots Prince Xiaobai's belt hook; the prince plays dead and becomes Duke Huan. *(TRADITIONAL — Shiji)* — comedy: "play dead… very dead".
3. **Story**: Bao Shuya: "to rule Qi, I suffice; to rule the world, you need Guan Zhong." Duke hires his would-be assassin — "best HR decision in history". *(TRADITIONAL, quote [verify])*
4. **Diagram — timeline**: 685 chancellor · 667 first hegemon covenant · 645 death.
5. **Problem**: empty treasury; in the *Guanzi* the duke lists taxes on buildings, trees, livestock, people; Guan Zhong rejects them — visible taxes breed resentment.
6. **Cliffhanger — diagram quote**: 唯官山海为可耳 → mountains = iron, seas = salt.

## Act 2 — The economics of a pinch of salt (part 2)
1. **Chapter card.**
2. **Caveat** (house rule): Guanzi edited c. 26 BC; Qingzhong chapters by later economists — "Guan Zhong brand". *(DEBATED)*
3. **Explainer**: monthly salt consumption 5⅓ / 3⅓ / 2⅓ sheng — everyone, every month.
4. **Diagram — flow**: salt works → state → tiny markup → ten million mouths → treasury.
5. **Diagram — stat**: 60,000,000 coins/month vs 30,000,000 from a head tax. *(rhetorical numbers)*
6. **Skit**: duke's eyes turn into coins; Guan Zhong: "they'll grumble about salt prices, not about you."
7. **Diagram — compare**: head tax vs hidden markup; "price-inelastic" explained. *(ANALOGY)*
8. **Explainer**: iron — needle, knife, plough: markup on every tool.
9. **Cliffhanger**: a painless money machine — what happens when it's turned all the way up?

## Act 3 — Two thousand years of salt (part 3)
1. **Chapter card.**
2. **Did it work?** Shiji: 通货积财，富国强兵; fish-and-salt profits; Qi becomes the first hegemon.
3. **Confucius**: criticised his extravagance, yet "without Guan Zhong we'd wear our hair loose and robes left-buttoned".
4. **Diagram — timeline**: 685 BC · c.119 BC Han monopoly · 81 BC debate · 758 Tang monopoly · 2017 reform.
5. **Story + skit**: Emperor Wu's wars; salt & iron tycoons run the monopoly; Sang Hongyang: "war needs money, money is in salt."
6. **Diagram — compare**: the Salt and Iron Debate (81 BC) — tease the next episode.
7. **Story**: Tang — salt ≈ half of revenue *[verify]*; smuggling; Huang Chao.
8. **Analogy**: gabelle and Gandhi's Salt March — tax salt and you tax everyone.
9. **Diagram — stat**: 2017 — China liberalises salt prices.
10. **Close**: genius or inventor of the stealth tax? Big idea: the easiest money is the money people don't notice paying. Tease EP02. Guan Zhong's last line: "You all still buy salt."
